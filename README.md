# The Guardians of Elaria

### The Complete Chronicle — a keepsake account and homebrew 5e expansion

The true story of three young heroes — **Lilly Glimmergear**, a deep gnome artificer; **Stabby Sharpblade**, a goblin monk; and **Ursa Catchum**, a star-druid — and the sleeping goddess who lights their way. Played across many evenings at a family table, set down here as an illustrated storybook you can read aloud, and a small expansion you can play from: borrow the creatures, hand out the relics, and carry the lantern further.

Read it in order. Each PDF is a chapter of the book.

## About this repository

The numbered PDFs above are the finished book. Everything else is the workshop behind it:

- `sessions/`, `frontmatter/`, `compendium/` — the editable source documents (Word)
- `assets/` — all illustrations, with `image_manifest.md` preserving every original filename and generation prompt
- `characters/` — current and historical character sheets
- `templates/` — the build system: `book_style.py` (the house style) and per-document build scripts; run `python templates/publish.py` to regenerate every PDF
- `memory/` — the campaign canon, session log, and house rules
- `CLAUDE.md` — the working rules for maintaining the archive

Ruleset: Dungeons & Dragons 2024 (revised fifth edition), with house rules in Compendium III. Made with love, for the kids who lived it.
