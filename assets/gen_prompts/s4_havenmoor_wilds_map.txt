Landscape orientation. THE FROZEN WILDS AROUND HAVENMOOR:
At the south edge on the coast, the snow-covered harbor town HAVENMOOR with a tall decorated pine in its square and a lighthouse labeled WYRMGALE LIGHTHOUSE on the point beside it.
North of town, three destinations hidden in the winter wilds, spread apart with pine forest, frozen ponds and snowy hills between them: FROSTPINE GLADE, a clearing of ice-armored pines with one huge blackened tree wrapped in black-ice chains, to the northwest; CRYSTALBROOK STREAM, a half-frozen brook with a small wooden bridge, to the north; THE WHISPERING HOLLOW, a ring of frost-covered standing stones in a hollow between two hills, to the northeast.
Faint wandering trails, ski and sled tracks, a wolf silhouette in the trees, snow falling. Kid-friendly storybook charm. No text other than the place names and compass.
