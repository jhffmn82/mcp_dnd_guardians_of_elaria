# Session Log

One short entry per session, newest last. Seeded during Phase 2 from existing documents; refine during Phase 3 canon extraction.

## Session 1: The Gathering of Friends
Eldridge Village. The party met at the Gilded Acorn Tavern and ventured toward the Forest of Whispers, encountering their first strange creatures (Cognifin, Lickgloom, Duckleaf). Party at level 3.

## Session 2: Oakshade Village
The party's reputation preceded them to Oakshade. Alwen the tavern keeper pointed them toward Professor Aelwyn and Ravenstone Laboratory to learn about the strange creatures and forest disturbances.

## Session 3: The Quest for Knowledge
Met Professor Aelwyn Ravenstone at his laboratory; he examined Lilly's Essence Sphere with awe. The party set out for the coastal town of Havenmoor.

## Session 4: Awakening to Shadows and Songs
Havenmoor, winter. Children abducted by Krampusshade; a haunting lullaby gripped the town. The party rescued Havenmoor's children and earned magic item rewards (Frostbite Shard Dagger among them).

## Session 5: The Curse of Davy Jones
Havenmoor saw the heroes off aboard the Stormwind under Captain Brynn Wavewarden. A storm capsized the ship; the Flying Dutchman rose beneath the wreck. The party discovered Davy Jones's essence-harvesting operation (crates stamped with the Gearhaven Artificers' Guild sigil), defeated him, and escaped as the Dutchman sank. Lilly infused a freed essence into Floraburst, who collapsed and began to change (petals "etched with frost and fire"; see CONTRADICTIONS C3 on the evolution chain).

## Session 6: Lanterns in the Fog (Wraithpine)
The fog-bound village of Wraithpine, where a False Hydra's song made everyone forget the people it ate. The party pieced the truth together (beeswax, bells, and black ribbons), destroyed the tavern head, and finished the creature in its lair beneath the Old Theater; the village's memories flooded back. In the lair a single chest bound in silver vines held Elaria's three relics: the Staff of Waking Constellations (Ursa), the Sash of the Swift Current (Stabby), and the Pocket Dynamo (Lilly). The companion emerged as Ghostbloom. No Session 7 followed; the next session was branded 8.

## Session 8: Gearhaven, the Clockwork City
The party reached Gearhaven and made the Gilded Gear home base. Guildmaster Vane revealed the Rift Portal ("Vane's Folly") and the Essence Sphere proved to be its missing anchor; Ursa read the orrery and revealed the anchor-stars going dark; Quill found Ash Catchum's trail ("east, to the old shrine"). Four fights: rogue market servitors, the Underworks rift swarm (Skitch the Bloodfang courier rescued; "come home" plea delivered), the Verdant Plaza tremor, and the Grand Custodian, freed with a gentle death that closed all of Gearhaven's rifts. Pikachu's projection appeared and warned of a vast hunger and its herald. Each hero chose among three "doors" for a solo year; Ghostbloom stayed with Vane. One year later: the party returns at level 7.
