# Session Log

One short entry per session, newest last. Seeded during Phase 2 from existing documents; refine during Phase 3 canon extraction.

## Session 1: The Gathering of Friends
Eldridge Village. The party met at the Gilded Acorn Tavern and ventured toward the Forest of Whispers, encountering their first strange creatures (Cognifin, Lickgloom, Duckleaf). Party at level 3.

## Session 2: Oakshade Village
The party's reputation preceded them to Oakshade. Alwen the tavern keeper pointed them toward Professor Aelwyn and Ravenstone Laboratory to learn about the strange creatures and forest disturbances.

## Session 3: The Quest for Knowledge
Met Professor Aelwyn Ravenstone at his laboratory; he examined Lilly's Essence Sphere with awe. The party set out for the coastal town of Havenmoor.

## Session 4: Awakening to Shadows and Songs
Havenmoor, winter. Children abducted by Krampusshade; a haunting lullaby gripped the town. The party rescued Havenmoor's children and earned magic item rewards (Frostbite Shard Dagger among them).

## Session 5: The Curse of Davy Jones
Havenmoor saw the heroes off. Under Captain Brynn Wavewarden they sailed for Gearhaven, but Davy Jones and his cursed ship tested them through a brutal night at sea. Floraburst absorbed a drifting ghost-essence soon after and evolved into Ghostbloom.

## Session 6: Lanterns in the Fog (Wraithpine)
The fog-bound village of Wraithpine, where a False Hydra's song made everyone forget the people it ate. The party broke the song, defeated the creature, and received their three relics from Wraithpine chests as Elaria's gift (Pocket Dynamo, Sash of the Swift Current, Staff of Waking Constellations). No Session 7 followed; the next session was branded 8.

## Session 8: Gearhaven, the Clockwork City
The party reached Gearhaven, met the Artificer's Guild and Vane, descended beneath the city, fought through swarms and the corrupted Custodian's Garden, and faced the Grand Custodian at the core. Party at level 5. A year-apart timeskip to level 7 is planned next.
