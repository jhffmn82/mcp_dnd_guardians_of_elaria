# House Rules and Standing Rulings

2024 (revised 5e) rules only; where 2014 and 2024 text conflict, 2024 wins. This list matches the one printed in the Compendium.

## House rules (adjudications beyond the printed rules)

1. Ursa's Starseed applies only to actual druid spells, NOT the Starry Form Archer luminous arrow. (The die is +1d4 as printed and **+1d8** once the Staff is awakened on the Reader of Omens road, which Ursa took.)
2. Potent Spellcasting rides the kindled staff once per turn (Ursa, from level 7; no double-dipping with a cantrip in the same turn).
3. **Arcane Firearm reaches delayed damage** (DM, 2026-09-04). Boomstick's 1d8 is rolled when Lilly casts, but it attaches to one damage roll of that spell *whenever* that roll actually happens. One die per cast, never more. This switches on **Web's burn** (one of the 2d4 rolls) and **Dragon's Breath** (Puff's 3d6 on a later turn). RAW sets no timing limit in either direction, so this is the table's reading, not an error in the printed feature.
4. **Burning webs deal 2d4 per creature, per round**, not per 5-ft cube (DM, 2026-09-04). A Huge creature standing in a dozen burning cubes still takes one 2d4. Per-cube would put roughly 18d4 of no-save damage on a Huge target from a 2nd-level slot.

- Lilly's spell list, 2024 legality pass (DM, 2026-08-13): Absorb Elements, Catapult, Snare and Tasha's Caustic Brew were removed. All four were 2014-era artificer spells and none appear on the 2024 Forge-of-the-Artificer list (verified on dnd2024.wikidot.com); her sheet was also preparing 7 where level 5 allows 6. Her prepared six are now Faerie Fire, Grease, Cure Wounds (1st) and Homunculus Servant, Dragon's Breath, Aid (2nd), with Shield, Thunderwave, Scorching Ray and Shatter still free from Artillerist. The Session 7 boss text no longer offers Absorb Elements as an answer to the Chain Lightning Burst: Shield still works, because the burst's primary target is an attack roll, and the arcs are Dex saves. **Superseded at level 7 (2026-08-17, commit 0b1e224): she prepares SEVEN, and the list now includes Web.** The live list is in `characters/builders/build_lilly.py`.
- **Arcane Firearm has no per-turn cap** (verified 2026-09-04 against dnd2024.wikidot.com/artificer:artillerist, "When you cast an Artificer spell through the firearm, roll 1d8"). The trigger is per qualifying CAST. `lilly_kit.md:125` and her sheet both said "each turn" and were corrected. In practice she casts one spell a turn, so it plays the same at the table. The THUNDER typing of the die is a campaign variant from Boomstick's own card (build_compendium.py); the printed feature grants an untyped bonus.
- **Casting a spell from a magic item does NOT trigger Arcane Firearm** (SRD 5.2.1 p.206, "Spells Cast from Items": an item-cast spell "requires no components unless the item's description notes otherwise"). No components means no Spellcasting Focus is involved, so the "through the firearm" trigger never fires. Web from the Wand of Web is **DC 13 with no 1d8**; Web from her own 2nd-level slot through Boomstick is **DC 16 with the 1d8**. The DC governs both the initial Dex save and the repeated Strength (Athletics) escape check the target can retry every turn, which is the real reason to spend the slot; the die is worth about 4.5 once.
- **Burning a Web frees whatever it was holding** (SRD 5.2.1: Restrained lasts "while in the webs", and a burned cube "burns away"). Her sheet briefly recommended Web-then-Flamethrower as an opening; that was wrong and was removed 2026-09-04. A 15-ft cone also clears only a handful of the cube's cells. The real synergy runs the other way: Restrained gives **Advantage on attacks against the target and Disadvantage on its Dex saves**, so a pinned enemy rolls the Flamethrower's DC 16 at Disadvantage and Force Ballista hits it with Advantage.
- Homunculus Servant, as played (DM, 2026-08-13): named PUFF by Lilly at the table. She ritual-casts it, so it costs NO spell slot, and the spell's duration is Instantaneous, so the homunculus is permanent until destroyed. Ritual casting locks it at spell level 2: AC 13, HP 15, Force Strike +8 for 1d6+2 force. It shares her initiative, acts immediately after her, and obeys with no action from her. Its design is DM-supplied (see assets/art_refs/REF_homunculus.png) and it is FAIRY-SIZED, small enough to sit in her two cupped hands.
