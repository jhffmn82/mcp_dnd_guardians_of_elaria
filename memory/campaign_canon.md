## Cosmology (locked by Session 7 + handoff; supersedes older lore)

- Five planes (Fire, Water, Air, Earth, Shadow) spin "like the gears of a great clock", held in alignment by ANCHOR-STARS. A star put out = a plane slips = a crack opens, leaking elemental Motes that madden machines and mutate creatures. The home world is the hub of the clock and has no anchor of its own.
- Star map (must match the Session 7 orrery plate, `assets/session_07/beat_3/orrery_reacts.png`, CANON per DM 2026-08-18, which shows THREE dark stars, one unlabeled): Fire/Ignis = **Aldebaran** (bright at S7; FLICKERING, UNDER ATTACK from S8's epilogue on, DM 2026-08-18); Water/Unda = **Fomalhaut** (same: flickering, under attack); Air/Aer = **Vega** (going dark); Earth/Terra = **Antares** (was two years dimming; RELIT in Session 8); Shadow/Umbra = **Algol** (dark); **Betelgeuse** = the keystone, the first star to fail in living memory (dimmed untended, see below), the one Ash chased; **Achernar** = the RESERVE anchor, held back against the worst, currently anchoring nothing; and **THE NAMELESS** (DM 2026-08-18, canonizing the unlabeled dark star in the orrery plate) = a star crossed off the Circle's list so long ago that no living sky-reader knows its name or what world it held. The Nameless is WHY the reserve exists. Its world was not saved. Nobody talks about it.
- **[LOCKED] BETELGEUSE IS THE KEYSTONE: the lock on Maldrith's prison.** Not one of the five elemental anchors, which is why it stands apart. Nobody snuffed it: it dimmed ON ITS OWN because the Warden sleeps and the lights went untended. Ash went east to investigate a failing lock, and a failing lock is exactly where the wall is thinnest. He was ruined by doing his job well. **It begins to visibly flicker and fade in Session 13** (DM 2026-08-18): the lock finally giving, which is the finale's clock.
- **The star map is a scoreboard, not scenery.** After Session 8 three anchors shine (Antares relit; Aldebaran and Fomalhaut flickering, both at once, which is not how stars die on their own: Nyxthid is attacking both). The Nameless hangs there as the quiet worst case.
- NYXTHID, the Harbinger of Decay (he): Maldrith's herald, actively PUTTING OUT the stars. Shadow magic, illusions, fear; partially in the Shadowfell. First glimpsed (unnamed) as the Dark Figure at Stormwatch Cliffs (Session 3).
  - **[DM ONLY, LOCKED 2026-08-17] NYXTHID IS ASH CATCHUM.** Ursa's father, corrupted, in full black, face concealed. NOT a fallen celestial (that description is retired DM-side; the lore primer's fallen-celestial paragraph stays AS DELIBERATE IN-WORLD MISBELIEF, framed as what the Guardians have glimpsed). He took the name Maldrith gave him and set down his own. He has known Ursa is his son since Stormwatch Cliffs, which is why the party has survived seven sessions of near-misses. Reveal at Session 12.
  - **Why Maldrith wanted him.** Only a sky-reader can find and reach an anchor-star (the far-east shrine is the door: see the endgame). She corrupted the best star-reader alive, because he was the only one who could put the lights out. His gift is her weapon.
- MALDRITH, the Weaver of Fractures (she): the true villain; primordial entropy imprisoned eons ago by the gods, Elaria foremost, after the War of Fractures. The "vast hunger pressing on the walls of every world" (Session 7).
  - **Her pitch, which is what corrupted Ash:** putting out the stars LIBERATES the planes. Chaos, yes, but beauty and magic pouring into a grey world; the party's own companions are her evidence, every one came through a crack.
  - **Why she is wrong:** it is not liberation, it is DECAY. Without the anchors, all of reality collapses, and decay does not stop at the beautiful parts. Ash can no longer see this.
  - **[LOCKED 2026-08-17] Maldrith put Elaria to sleep**, as the last wall closed: a revenge that takes eons. Unrevealed to the players until the finale. The in-world verses saying Elaria slept from exhaustion are what mortals believe.
  - **NAME KNOWLEDGE (corrected 2026-08-17):** the players HAVE heard "Maldrith" (Lore of Elaria boxes, S2-S4, and the primer). "Nyxthid" is first spoken in-fiction in Session 8's shrine vision, per the endgame table; before that it appeared only in a S7 DM-side parenthetical.
- ELARIA, the sleeping Warden of Balance, "tended the lights of heaven." Guides via visions (Whispering Glade, Session 2) and star-marked gifts (seven-point star sigil).
- The Enchanted Potatoes: three, carried by Ursa, left by Ash nine years ago. Old lore names them Potato of Healing, Potato of Transformation, Potato of Insight; "remnants of a shattered covenant" (Elaria's own essence, bound as safeguards). **Ash left his infant son the three pieces needed to fix what he was walking toward**, without knowing it.
- Rifts cannot be closed from our side; each fraying world has a corrupted GUARDIAN; free the guardian and that world's cracks close (proven on the Grand Custodian, Session 7).
- The Rift Portal ("Vane's Folly"): ten years of Vane's work, missing an anchor vessel; the Essence Sphere is that anchor. **The Sphere (thread 3, narrowed 2026-08-17): Poots did NOT build it. She FOUND an ancient gatherer of unknown make, recognised it, and built the Glimmerspire brass housing around it.** The open mystery is who made it and how it reached Glimmerspire. (Volumes 02/09 carry the older "Glimmerspire-forged" telling as in-world belief.)

## The endgame (LOCKED 2026-08-17; S13-S14 expanded by DM ruling 2026-08-18)

**The engine.** Each fraying world has a corrupted GUARDIAN (its legendary); freeing rather than killing it closes that world's cracks; the Staff of Waking Constellations relights the anchor-star afterward. One designed companion comes home per elemental rift.

**Why they visit planes whose stars still burn.** After Session 8, Aelwyn works out that the GUARDIAN is twisted FIRST, before the star fails. Securing a still-lit plane's Guardian denies Ash that star entirely. Per the 2026-08-18 flicker ruling both remaining lit anchors are already under attack from S8's epilogue on, so **Sessions 9 and 10 are races, not pre-emptive strikes**; 11 and 12 are salvage on the two he already owns.

**Session 14, the finale at the old shrine (DM ruling 2026-08-18 sites and motivates the locked three-phase structure).** The party crosses the Skyless Country on Ash's cairn road, the road Ursa repaired, and reaches the shrine where Elaria showed him the three things. **The shrine is how a sky-reader reaches the stars: it is where Ash crossed to Betelgeuse nine years ago.** There they perform the ritual: the waking of Elaria. Nyxthid comes to stop them, and to finish it: with Betelgeuse guttering he is preparing to unleash Maldrith. Then the locked three phases run:

1. **The Father.** Elaria wakes but is SPENT; she can only witness. They defeat Nyxthid, and in losing, **Ash surfaces**, himself for about a minute, long enough to say the thing he has not been able to say for three years. **Then Maldrith takes the body, and that is what kills him.**
   - **[DM PROTECTION] She does not wear his face.** At possession the body is consumed and RESHAPED into her Avatar, enormous and not remotely human. Ash's death is the last thing that happens to Ash.
2. **The Unwinnable One.** The Avatar takes the party apart. The scripted loss gets an objective that is not "win": survive three rounds, or get the Sphere to the anchor point before falling. They fail to beat her and SUCCEED at the thing that matters.
3. **The Circle.** Unconscious and dying, **the Sphere empties itself**: every companion they ever gathered comes out at once (Ghostbloom, the four elementals, Pikachu) and forms a ring around three unconscious children. Maldrith's own evidence chooses the other side. Inside the circle they meet Elaria, who grants each hero the grown-up version of their Whispering Glade boon (Ursa: Planar Awareness; Lilly: the Sphere's Planar Attunement; Stabby: Glimpse of Possibility, "because the goddess smiled at the goblin"): same goddess, same three gifts, twelve sessions of growth between. Then they get up and seal her.

**The seal.** Relight BETELGEUSE, the keystone, using the three enchanted potatoes. The permanent fix is the waking: stars go out because the Warden sleeps, and this time she stays awake.

**The epilogue.** The survivors of Gearhaven regroup (resolved kindly: Vane, Henna, Quill, Skitch and the rest accounted for). **Ash and Elaria appear together to the party**, Ash as a spirit, whole and no longer in black; the thanks is specifically for the redemption: they did not defeat Ash, they GOT HIM BACK. The portal is not rebuilt; the sealed sky does not need one.
