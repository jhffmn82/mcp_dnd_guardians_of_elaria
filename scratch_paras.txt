4 'The Big Picture'
5 'The premise: The planar boundaries are failing. The planes are held in alignment by anchor-stars; when a star goes dark, its plane slips and a crack opens, leaking elemental Motes into the world. The goddess Elaria — a sleeping Warden of Balance who once ‘tended the lights of heaven’ — guides the party through visions and star-marked gifts. Behind the chaos: Nyxthid, a harbinger who is PUTTING OUT THE STARS to collapse the planes and clear the way for the ancient Maldrith to awaken. The party must gather the Motes, relight the dark stars, restore the safeguards (Ash Catchum’s enchanted potatoes), and seal the rift before the worlds unravel.'
6 'The shape: After Wraithpine, the party reaches Gearhaven, the clockwork city, where Guildmaster Vane has spent ten years building a Rift Portal but lacked its heart — a vessel that can survive and gather planar energy. Lilly’s Essence Sphere is exactly that. Gearhaven becomes home base (the hub); each elemental plane becomes a survival-mode dungeon run (a rift). Return with a Mote, install it, grow stronger, unlock the next rift. Collect them all → reach Nyxthid → final showdown.'
7 'Two modes of play'
8 'HUB (roleplay-forward): Gearhaven between runs — NPCs, choices, crafting, story, Sphere upgrades, light encounters. Safe. Long rests allowed.'
9 'RIFTS (combat-forward survival): step through the portal and the gloves come off — NO long rests, attrition, random encounters, an elemental boss guarding the Mote. The contrast is the point.'

11 'Locked Cosmology'
12 'Established in Session 8 via Vane’s orrery and Ursa’s revelation. This is now canon and threads through every rift.'
13 'Star-Anchors: each plane hangs from an anchor-star. A dark star = a slipped plane = a crack. The cracks follow the sky going out, constellation by constellation — not random decay.'
14 'Nyxthid’s signature: he is snuffing the anchor-stars. Wherever a star goes dark, expect a crack, a leak, a rift.'
15 'Elaria, the sleeping Warden: goddess of Balance who kept the stars lit. While she sleeps, they can be put out. (What put her to sleep = unrevealed mystery.)'
16 'The Staff of Waking Constellations = counter-weapon: lights a NEW star after every major victory — literally relighting what Nyxthid snuffs. Ursa carries the one thing built to fight back.'
17 'Ash Catchum followed the dark stars: Ursa’s father tracked the sky going out, hunting the cause; headed ‘east, to the old shrine.’'
18 'The Sphere mystery: forged by the Glimmerspire masters BEFORE the crisis — the cure built before the disease. How did Poots / the masters KNOW? (ties to Elaria’s foresight; pays off via Lilly’s arc and the finale.)'

22 'Key Campaign Mechanics'
23 '1. The Essence Sphere is a ‘box’ (and a gatherer)'
24 'It holds Pikachu’s spark and actively PULLS loose motes (revealed in S8). After its Gearhaven upgrade it holds multiple sparks — so the party can catch wild creatures in the rifts and store them. Ursa’s Mark of Handling + Animal Friendship make him the catcher. Caught creatures = a reserve roster for the no-long-rest survival runs. NOTE: Vane NEVER keeps the Sphere — he tunes it on the bench and hands it right back. Pikachu is never at risk.'
25 '2. Motes = power-ups + keys'
26 'Each elemental boss drops a Mote. Installing it: (a) grants a small permanent party-wide boon (Earth→resistance/stoneskin; Water→water-breathing+heal tick; etc.), and (b) unlocks the next rift. If Lilly took the Mote-Channeling path, installed motes also fuel her discharge menu. Collect-and-grow.'
27 '3. Wild creatures vs. Elemental Bosses'
28 'Wild creatures (Frostfern, Thundercrackle, etc.): fight, befriend, or catch into the Sphere.'
29 'Elemental Bosses (Terranox-type): the rift’s ‘gym leader,’ not catchable, guards the Mote. One big set-piece per rift.'
30 '4. The one-year time skip'
31 'S8 ends with the portal still being built (it takes ~a year) and the party splitting up. Each returns at Level 7, changed and powered up, with a personal ‘who I became’ story. The reunion is the cold open of S9. Justifies the level jump without grind.'
