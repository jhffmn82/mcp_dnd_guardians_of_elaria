# Wither and Bloom

**Source: Arcana Unleashed (release 1 Sep 2026). Text supplied by the DM from the
book on 2026-08-24 and transcribed here verbatim.** This is NOT SRD content and is
NOT on dnd2024.wikidot.com (checked 2026-08-24, the whole druid list: absent).
Recorded so the campaign has a citable source rather than anyone's memory.

**Level 2 Necromancy (Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard)**

- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a withered vine twisted into a loop)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You invoke both death and life in a 10-foot-radius Sphere centered on a point
within range. Each creature of your choice in that area makes a Constitution
saving throw, taking 3d6 Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage
on a successful one. Nonmagical plants in that area that aren't creatures, such
as trees and shrubs, wither.

In addition, one creature of your choice in that area can roll one of its
unexpended Hit Point Dice and regain a number of Hit Points equal to the roll
plus your spellcasting ability modifier. That die is then expended.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each spell
slot level above 2. The number of Hit Point Dice that can be rolled and expended
increases by one for each spell slot level above 2. You add your spellcasting
modifier to the number of Hit Points regained by this spell only once, regardless
of the number of Hit Point Dice rolled and expended.

## Why it suits Ursa (measured, 200-seed sweeps)

**Instantaneous, so no Concentration.** This is the whole reason it fits: Conjure
Animals owns his concentration for the entire day, which makes every other useful
2nd-level druid spell (Spike Growth, Moonbeam, Summon Beast, Hold Person, Flaming
Sphere) uncastable while the pack is out. Only Aid and Barkskin were otherwise
available, and Lilly already has Aid.

**Allies are never caught** ("each creature of your choice"), so he can drop it on
a melee scrum containing Stabby.

Measured at DC 16 against Session 8's enemies, it lands 1.5 to 2.7 casts a day
catching 3.0 to 3.8 enemies each, and it beats Barkskin everywhere:
                     NET taken  knockdowns  floor  wipes/200
  nothing (Piplup)         111        0.06    81%          0
  Barkskin                 116        0.02    83%          0
  Wither and Bloom         105        0.02    85%          0
  --- Chimchar at x1.5 enemy HP ---
  nothing                  258        1.11    17%         15
  Barkskin                 260        1.19     4%         17
  Wither and Bloom         246        0.93    19%         12
It also SCALES: 2.0 casts a day at normal difficulty, 2.7 under pressure, because
harder fights keep enemies bunched longer. Barkskin gets worse under pressure.

**Do not upcast it for the healing.** The modifier is added only once no matter
how many dice are rolled, and a 3rd-level slot is a Conjure Animals cast.

**The heal costs the TARGET a Hit Die**, which is short-rest healing spent early.
The simulator models a real 7-die budget per hero so that trade is honest.
