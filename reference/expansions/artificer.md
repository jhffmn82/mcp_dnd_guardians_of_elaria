## Class features (mechanics, summarized)
- **Spellcasting (L1).** Int-based. Casts through tools (Thieves'/Tinker's/Artisan's) as the focus, so Artificer spells gain an M component. Prepared caster: prepares a list, changeable on a Long Rest. Recommended cantrips Acid Splash + Prestidigitation; recommended L1 Cure Wounds + Grease.
- **Tinker's Magic (L1).** Knows Mending. As a Magic action with Tinker's Tools, conjure one mundane item from a set list (rope, torch, caltrops, net, etc.) within 5 ft; it lasts until a Long Rest. Uses = Int mod (min 1) per Long Rest.
- **Replicate Magic Item (L2).** Learns magic-item "plans" (4 to start, more by level). On a Long Rest, craft up to your Magic Items-column number of items from known plans, with Tinker's Tools; can attune instantly on creation. Items are impermanent (vanish 1d4 days after you die, or if you replace the plan). Any Wand or Weapon you make can serve as a Spellcasting Focus. Plan lists are tiered by Artificer level (2+, 6+, ...). Notable L2 plans: Bag of Holding, Sending Stones, Wand of the War Mage +1, Repeating Shot, Shield +1, Weapon +1.
- **Magic Item Tinker (L6).** Adds three Bonus Action / Magic action tricks to Replicate: **Charge** (spend a slot to recharge a created item's charges), **Drain** (destroy a created item for a slot: L1 if Common, L2 if Uncommon/Rare; 1/Long Rest), **Transmute** (reshape a created item into another known plan; 1/Long Rest).
- **Flash of Genius (L7).** *Reaction.* When you or a creature within 30 ft fails an ability check or saving throw, add your Int mod (min +1) to the roll. Uses = Int mod (min 1) per Long Rest.
- **Magic Item Adept (L10) / Master (L18).** Attune to 4 / 6 items.
- **Spell-Storing Item (L11).** On a Long Rest, store a L1-3 Artificer spell (action casting time, no consumed material) in a weapon or focus. Any holder can produce it with a Magic action using your spell mod; usable twice per Int mod before it empties.
- **Advanced Artifice (L14).** Attune to 5 items; regain one Flash of Genius use on a Short Rest.
- **Soul of Artifice (L20).** Cheat Death (disintegrate Uncommon/Rare created items at 0 HP to set HP to 20 x items destroyed); regain all Flash of Genius on a Short Rest if attuned to any item.

# Artillerist (subclass, 2024)

Lilly Glimmergear's subclass. Long-range energy and the Eldritch Cannon.

## Subclass features (mechanics, summarized)
- **Tools of the Trade (L3).** Proficiency with **Martial Ranged weapons** (this is where a firearm proficiency legitimately comes from if the DM treats firearms as martial ranged), Woodcarver's Tools, and Wands craft in half the time.
- **Artillerist Spells (L3), always prepared:**

| Artificer level | Spells |
|---|---|
| 3 | Shield, Thunderwave |
| 5 | Scorching Ray, Shatter |
| 9 | Fireball, Wind Wall |
| 13 | Ice Storm, Wall of Fire |
| 17 | Cone of Cold, Wall of Force |

- **Eldritch Cannon (L3).** Magic action with Smith's or Woodcarver's Tools to make a Small/Tiny cannon within 5 ft. One at a time; one per Long Rest unless you spend a spell slot for more. Lasts 1 hour or until 0 HP; dismiss as a Magic action.

  **Eldritch Cannon stat block:**
  - AC 18; HP = **5 x your Artificer level** (Mending restores 2d6); Immune Poison, Psychic.
  - **Activate (Bonus Action, you within 60 ft):** the cannon may move up to 15 ft before or after, and uses ONE mode, chosen fresh each activation:
    - **Flamethrower:** 15-ft Cone, Dex save vs your spell DC, 2d8 Fire (half on save).
    - **Force Ballista:** ranged spell attack from the cannon at a target within 120 ft, 2d8 Force and push 5 ft.
    - **Protector:** the cannon and chosen creatures within 10 ft gain 1d8 + Int (min 1) temp HP.
- **Arcane Firearm (L5).** On a Long Rest, carve sigils into a Rod, Staff, Wand, or **Martial Ranged weapon** to make it your Arcane Firearm (one at a time). Use it as a Spellcasting Focus; when you cast an Artificer spell through it, add **1d8** to one of the spell's damage rolls.
- **Explosive Cannon (L9).** **Detonate** (Reaction when the cannon takes damage, within 60 ft: destroy it, 20-ft radius Dex save, 3d10 Force, half on save). **Firepower:** cannon damage and Protector temp HP each +1d8.
- **Fortified Position (L15).** Two cannons at once (create/activate both together); you and allies within 10 ft of a cannon have Half Cover.

## How this maps to Lilly (campaign cross-reference)
- **The Sentinel** (her relic, `compendium`) is a homebrew UPGRADE of the base Eldritch Cannon: HP "5 + five times your Artificer level" (40 at L7) vs the base cannon's 5 x level (35 at L7), it acts on its own and is permanent, and its modes are reworked (Force Blast / Electric Pulse / Guardian Protocol). Treat the Sentinel's printed card as authoritative for her; this entry is the RAW baseline it was built from.
- **The mode-per-activation house-rule note in CLAUDE.md is actually RAW** here: Activate lets you pick Flamethrower / Force Ballista / Protector fresh every Bonus Action.
- **Boomstick as Arcane Firearm:** a repeating pistol is a Martial Ranged weapon, so it qualifies as the Arcane Firearm carrier, granting the +1d8 to a spell's damage. This is the basis of her True Strike + 1d8 line.
- **Flash of Genius x5** = her Int modifier (+5) uses per Long Rest, exactly as simmed.
- **Firearm proficiency:** comes from Tools of the Trade (Martial Ranged), pending the DM's ruling that firearms count as martial ranged. No feat or dip needed.
