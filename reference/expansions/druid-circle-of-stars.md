# Druid, Circle of the Stars (2024 PHB) — Ursa's subclass

Base Druid is in the SRD (`reference/srd/04_classes_druid_fighter_monk.md`). This file covers only the subclass, plus the two base-Druid facts that were repeatedly misremembered.

## Base-Druid facts worth pinning (from the SRD, verified)
- **Level 7: Elemental Fury**, choose one:
  - **Potent Spellcasting.** Add your Wisdom modifier to the damage of any Druid cantrip. (This is the option Ursa uses; +5 at Wis 20. Applies to Druid cantrips like Starry Wisp, Thorn Whip, Produce Flame.)
  - **Primal Strike.** Once per turn on a weapon or Wild Shape attack hit, +1d8 Cold/Fire/Lightning/Thunder.
- **Wild Shape uses at level 7 = 3.** Regain one on a Short Rest, all on a Long Rest. (Starry Form spends one of these.)

## Subclass features (mechanics, summarized)

### Level 3: Star Map
A Tiny object, usable as a Druid Spellcasting Focus. While holding it: you have **Guidance and Guiding Bolt** always prepared, and you can cast **Guiding Bolt without a spell slot**, a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (min 1) per Long Rest. (At Wis 20 that is 5 free Guiding Bolts/day.) Lose it and a 1-hour ceremony on a rest makes a new one.

### Level 3: Starry Form
Bonus Action, **expend a Wild Shape use** (not shape-shifting). Lasts 10 minutes; ends on Incapacitated, dismissal, or reuse. Sheds Bright Light 10 ft + Dim Light 10 ft more. You keep your own statistics. Choose one constellation when you assume it:
- **Archer.** On activation and as a **Bonus Action** each later turn, make a ranged spell attack (a luminous arrow) at one creature within 60 ft: on a hit, **1d8 + Wisdom modifier Radiant** damage. (Becomes 2d8 + Wis at level 10.)
- **Chalice.** When you cast a spell with a slot that restores HP, you or a creature within 30 ft regains **1d8 + Wisdom modifier** HP. (2d8 + Wis at level 10.)
- **Dragon.** When you make an Int or Wis check, or a **Constitution save to maintain Concentration**, treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10. (The concentration-guard form.)

### Level 6: Cosmic Omen
On a Long Rest, roll a die; until your next Long Rest you gain a Reaction based on even/odd:
- **Weal (even):** when a creature you can see within 30 ft is about to make a D20 Test, Reaction to **add 1d6** to the roll.
- **Woe (odd):** same trigger, Reaction to **subtract 1d6**.
Uses = Wisdom modifier (min 1) per Long Rest. Note the trigger is "**about to make**" the roll (declare before it is rolled), and it works on **any** creature you can see (allies to boost, enemies to hinder).
- [DM CHECK, self-target] The 2024 wording is "a creature you can see within 30 feet of you," which unlike the 2014 "another creature" is ambiguous about whether Ursa can target his own D20 Test. A boss sim hit exactly this. Treat as a DM ruling; the safe reading is others-only.

### Level 10: Twinkling Constellations
Archer and Chalice dice become 2d8; Dragon grants Fly 20 (hover); you may change the glimmering constellation at the start of each turn.

### Level 14: Full of Stars
While in Starry Form, you are partially incorporeal: Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing.

## Campaign cross-reference (Ursa at level 7)
Has: Star Map (free Guiding Bolts), Starry Form (all three constellations available, one per activation), Cosmic Omen, Elemental Fury (Potent Spellcasting, +5 to Druid cantrip damage), 3 Wild Shape uses.

- **"Starseed +1d4" is a CAMPAIGN HOMEBREW rider, not official Circle of the Stars.** Official RAW has no Starseed; the Archer luminous arrow is 1d8 + Wis with no extra die. The house rule (`memory/house_rules.md`) says Starseed's +1d4 rides actual Druid spells (Guiding Bolt, Moonbeam, Starry Wisp) but NOT the Archer arrow. Keep the two straight: when simming, the Archer arrow is 1d8 + Wis, and Starseed adds +1d4 only to true Druid-spell casts.
- **Potent Spellcasting on the kindled staff once per turn** is house rule #2, layered on top of the RAW Potent Spellcasting above.
- **Starlit Channeler / Warden's Mantle** (the path-reward door) is a campaign homebrew built on this subclass; its printed compendium card is authoritative for that road.
