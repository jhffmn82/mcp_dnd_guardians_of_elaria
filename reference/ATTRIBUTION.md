# Reference Rules: sources and licensing

## SRD 5.2.1 (the `srd/` folder and `SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf`)

This material includes content from the **System Reference Document 5.2.1**
("SRD 5.2.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at
https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2.1 is licensed under the
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License** (CC-BY-4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

The files in `srd/` are a faithful transcription of the SRD 5.2.1 into
markdown for search and reference. The PDF is the authoritative source; if
the markdown ever disagrees with it, the PDF wins.

## Expansions (the `expansions/` folder)

Content beyond the SRD (Artificer, the Peace Domain, firearms, and other
subclasses) is NOT open-licensed. The files here are **concise factual
summaries of game mechanics in our own words, with citations** to where the
full text lives (the 2024 wiki at dnd2024.wikidot.com and the relevant
official books). They deliberately do not reproduce the copyrighted prose.
Where a mechanic could not be verified against a source it is marked
[UNVERIFIED, check the book].

## Why this exists

Claude was stating 2024 rules from unreliable memory and getting them wrong.
This folder is the authoritative source it must consult (see the standing
rule in CLAUDE.md) instead of guessing. Note: WebFetch cannot reach the wiki
(it loops on the site's HTTP/HTTPS redirect); use the in-app browser
(preview_start + navigate + get_page_text) instead.
