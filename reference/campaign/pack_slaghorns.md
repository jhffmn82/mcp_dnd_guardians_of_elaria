# THE SLAGHORN PACK (4x Underroot Slaghorn), Session 8+ encounter

Four rift-corrupted burrowing bulls of the Underroot, hunting as a wedge.
NO vulnerabilities of any kind (specifically: not vulnerable to Lightning or
Thunder; they take normal damage from Lilly's whole kit). This is the
action-economy parity answer: 4 enemy units vs the party's 4.

## UNDERROOT SLAGHORN (each of the four; name them A, B, C, D)

Large Elemental (planar-corrupted beast), unaligned
AC 16 (slag hide)   HP 130 each   Speed 50 ft., burrow 30 ft.
STR 20 (+5)  DEX 12 (+1)  CON 18 (+4)  INT 4 (-3)  WIS 12 (+1)  CHA 6 (-2)
Saves Str +8, Con +7   PB +3
Resistances bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks
Immunities poison; Poisoned
Vulnerabilities NONE
Senses darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 30 ft.; passive Perception 11

### Traits
- **Pack Wedge.** The Slaghorn has Advantage on attack rolls against a creature
  if at least one other Slaghorn is within 5 feet of that creature.
- **Corrupted.** Counts as planar-corrupted (Stabby's Cleansing Edge applies).
- **Slag Shudder.** The first time each fight a Slaghorn is reduced below half
  HP (65), its hide cracks and vents: each creature within 5 ft makes a DC 14
  Dex save, 2d6 Fire on a failure, half on a success.

### Actions
- **Gore.** +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., 2d10+5 Piercing.
- **Trample Charge.** If it moved 20+ ft straight toward the target this turn:
  Gore at +8 for 2d10+5 Piercing, and the target (Large or smaller) makes a
  DC 15 Str save or falls Prone. If the target falls Prone, the Slaghorn can
  make one Stomp against it as part of the same action: +8, 2d6+5 Bludgeoning.

### Bonus Actions
- **Dust Snort (1/fight each).** 10-ft cone of hot grit: each creature in it
  makes a DC 14 Con save or is Blinded until the start of the Slaghorn's next
  turn.

## Pack tactics (run them like animals, not soldiers)
- They open with a four-wide Trample Charge line at whoever is in front.
- Pack Wedge means they WANT to double up on one target; two pairs.
- A Slaghorn below half (Slag Shudder spent) tries to circle for a fresh
  charge lane instead of standing in melee.
- One of the four (D, the young one) breaks off toward the BACKLINE (Ursa or
  Lilly) on round 2 if the front line holds: the sims keep showing Lilly ends
  fights unspent, so the pack must make her spend.
- Epic-not-lethal: they are corrupted animals; when the last one drops below
  20 HP the corruption gutters and it flees/collapses rather than dying on a
  hero's blade if the table would rather free it (DM's call in narration).

## Sim verdict (2026-08, audited)
- **Fight length: 5 rounds, on target.** Party output ran ~104/round against
  520 pack HP; semi-focused fire (never all four on one bull) is what kept it
  in the window. The one bull nobody focused still had 11 HP at the end.
- **The party barely bled.** Sandshrew's taunt-tank loop (Challenge + Curl Up
  10 + nonmagical resist + AC 17) turned roughly 90+ points of thrown gores
  into ~26 taken; only a crit and the fire Shudders moved its HP. Dust Snort
  (Blind) was the pack's best tech, twice. Caution: vs magical attackers or
  anything cold, Sandshrew's wall flips hard.
- **Lilly finished a FOURTH straight sim spending nothing** (zero slots, zero
  reactions). The D-peel died 20 ft short: Stunning-slow plus Earthquake
  rubble quartered its approach while the whole party focused it.
- **Earthquake earned its slot twice over** (full-damage-on-save beats the
  resist wall, and the rubble stopped the peel). Electric Pulse fired once
  for little; reaction-lock is wasted on reaction-less animals.
- What played great: the peel bending the party's whole round 2 around it,
  Lilly's signature True Strike kill on D, Stabby's Devour finisher with the
  Focus refund, Ursa's arrow taking C at range.

## v2 changes for the real table (from the sim)
1. **The peeler BURROWS.** D (burrow 30) goes UNDER the field on round 2, no
   opportunity attacks, no rubble, and surfaces next to Lilly. Two peelers
   (C and D) is the stronger lever if the front line is holding easily.
2. Let the peeler Dust Snort the first threat it passes.
3. State the size (Large) at the table: Stabby's Fury of the Small needs a
   larger target, and the audit could not verify it from the setup line.
4. Epic-not-lethal close is written into the pack: the last Slaghorn below
   20 HP gutters and collapses; freeing it beats killing it.
